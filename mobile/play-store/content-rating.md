# Content rating questionnaire answers

Fill this in under **App content → Content ratings** in Play Console. This will give you an IARC rating.

Category: **News**

Violence: No. The app aggregates published news headlines and summaries; we do not produce graphic content ourselves. Publishers occasionally cover violent events. Answer **No** to all questions about user-generated violence, sexual violence, depictions of self-harm.

Sexuality: No.

Profanity: No (summaries are written by an LLM with a neutral wire-style tone).

Controlled substances: No.

User generated content: No.

Users can interact: No. The app has no comments, follows, chat, or messaging.

Shares user location: No.

Personal information sharing: No.

Digital purchases: No.

Unrestricted internet: No. The app loads a fixed JSON feed and opens links in the system browser; there is no browsing surface inside the app.

Expected outcome rating: **Everyone** in most regions, **PEGI 3** in EU, **3+** in IARC, **L** in Brazil.
