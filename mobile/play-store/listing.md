## Full description (max 4000 characters)

GeoPulse is a quiet, hourly geopolitics front page for people who want to follow the world without drowning in it.

Every hour, GeoPulse pulls fresh stories from a wide set of public news sources, ranks them, and writes short plain-English summaries so you can catch up in a couple of minutes. No infinite feed. No algorithmic outrage loop. No ads anywhere. You open the app, see what changed in the last hour, and get on with your day.

What you get

A single front page of the most important geopolitics stories right now, refreshed every hour on the hour. Concise summaries written for clarity, not for clicks. A built in archive of every previous hourly edition, so you can scroll back through how a story actually evolved. A Hindi edition alongside English, switchable from the header. Light mode, dark mode, and thirteen accent colours so the app looks the way you want it to. Pull to refresh, an offline cache of the last edition you opened, and tap through to the original publisher when you want the full piece.

How it works

GeoPulse fetches publicly available news data from the same feed that powers pulse.lavkesh.com. The newsletter is generated automatically by a public pipeline that runs every hour on GitHub Actions. You can read the source code, see exactly which sources are used, and watch the ranking and summarisation logic on GitHub. Nothing in the app tracks you. There is no account, no login, no personalisation profile, no analytics SDK.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, the app subscribes you to a single topic that fires when a new hourly edition is published with fresh stories. You can turn this off in your device settings at any time. We use Firebase Cloud Messaging from Google to deliver the alert. No personal data is attached to the message.

What this app is not

GeoPulse is not a social network. There are no comments, no likes, no follows. It is not a personalised feed. Every reader sees the same front page. It is not a wire service or an original reporting outlet. Every story links back to the publisher who actually did the work.

Who it is for

Anyone who wants a calm, twice or thrice a day glance at what is happening in the world without giving up an hour of attention to a news app. Commuters, researchers, students, analysts, and anyone tired of being shouted at by their phone.

Privacy

GeoPulse does not collect personal information. There is no account system. We do not share data with third parties. The only data leaving your device is the network request to fetch the current edition and, if you opt in, your Firebase Cloud Messaging registration token used to deliver new edition alerts. Full policy at https://pulse.lavkesh.com/privacy/

Open source

The site, the build pipeline, and the app shell are all open source. Fork the project at github.com/lavkeshlav/geo-pulse and run your own edition.
